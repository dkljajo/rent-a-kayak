Explore Mostar from the Water

Kayak rental on the beautiful Neretva River. Enjoy nature, relax, and discover hidden gems of Herzegovina. Lake Mostar is only 10 minutes away driving north of the city . It is about 12 km long . In a short timeyou can truly enjoy being on the water surronded by nature that is still preserved. The advantage of this lake is the calmnes that mostly prevails on it.

Hiking

HIKING - Diva Grabovica- Zlijeb-Tise, Čvrsnica - Visit canyon of river “Bijela” Immediately after the village of Grabovica, the hiking trail plunges into a dense, first hornbeam, then beech forest and climbs up to the hiking house "Zlijeb". Five minutes from the mountain house, there is a viewpoint from which one of the most beautiful views of the valley and Veliki Kuk can be seen. The tour is not overly demanding, the trail is excellent, nicely marked and cleaned. The starting point is at 260m, and the finish at 1090m. It takes about 2 hours of easy ascent, where you can feel the real mountain, considering the environment itself. There are two sources of drinking water on the trail, standard hiking equipment is required Includes: Transport A meal on the mountain Does not include: Insurance Drink The number of participants is unlimited.
